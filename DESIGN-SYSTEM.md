## Brand & Style

The design system embodies nocturnal luxury, rhythmic discipline, and tailored exclusivity. Designed for an elite social dance club and studio in Bergen, the digital aesthetic pairs the kinetic sensuality of mambo with Nordic architectural restraint. It balances the raw velvet atmosphere of an upscale private members' lounge with the polished precision of world-class Latin dance education.

The design movement is **High-Contrast Minimalist Luxury**:

- Expansive, breathing negative space that gives content an editorial, gallery-like weight.
- Uncompromising dark theme rooted in pitch black, layered charcoal planes, and warm metallic accents.
- Severe, elegant geometry counterbalanced by warm golden lighting metaphors and subtle tactile borders.
- Cinematic, high-energy imagery framed with restrained, architectural typography.

## Colors

The palette draws from nocturnal club culture and warm metallic patinas:

- **Deep Black (`#080808`)**: The foundational canvas. Replaces standard interface grays with absolute nocturnal depth.
- **Charcoal Black (`#1A1A1A`)**: Surface foundation for containers, layered cards, and navigation overlays.
- **Muted Charcoal (`#121212`)**: Secondary surface tier for sunken modules and segmented control tracks.
- **Cream White (`#F4F1EA`)**: High-legibility editorial primary text. Warm undertones prevent harsh optical vibrations against pitch black.
- **Primary Accent / Mambo Gold (`#D39D3B`)**: Applied intentionally to dominant headline accents, primary call-to-actions, active indicators, and high-tier VIP badges.
- **Secondary Accent / Champagne (`#F0D6AD`)**: Refined luminescence for subheadings, ghost borders, metadata chips, and secondary CTAs.
- **Hover / Bronze Depth (`#8F6327`)**: Interactive press states, active depth shadows, and rich baseline borders.
- **Muted Border (`#26231E`)**: Low-contrast structural border with a faint golden undertone for card separators and hairline dividers.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes distinct theatrical tension:

- **Headings (`Bebas Neue`)**: Tall, architectural, bold, and strictly uppercase. Headings command the vertical axis, invoking tall stage prosceniums and vintage nightclub marquees. Letter spacing remains wide to maintain legibility.
- **Subheadings, Navigation & Actions (`Montserrat`)**: Uppercase with aggressive letter spacing (`0.15em` to `0.22em`). Provides structured, modern precision that cuts through dark backgrounds.
- **Body (`Inter`)**: Rendered in Cream White (`#F4F1EA`) with relaxed line heights (`1.65` to `1.75`) to allow unhurried reading of class schedules, instructor dossiers, and event descriptions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined, architectural 12-column grid system that emphasizes intentional asymmetry and breathing space:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid, max width `80rem` (1280px), centered with `4rem` lateral margins and `2rem` gutters. Section paddings range from `5rem` to `8rem` to create dramatic breaks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Elements collapse from 3-column structures to alternating 2-column rhythms.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px)**: 4-column grid with `1.25rem` outer margin and `1rem` gutters. Headlines adapt to dedicated fluid scales, while horizontal margins protect thumb sweep areas.

Content density stays intentionally low: elements must never feel crowded. Spacing multiples revolve around an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`, `3rem`, `4rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical stage metaphors—layering dark surfaces, rim lighting, and warm gold glows rather than generic gray drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Background Canvas)**: Solid `#080808`. The infinite baseline.
- **Level 1 (Structural Surfaces & Cards)**: `#1A1A1A` with a delicate `1px` stroke of `#26231E` or `rgba(240, 214, 173, 0.12)`. No shadow, pure perimeter definition.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Elements & Modals)**: `#1F1F1F` elevated using a subtle golden ambient plume: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(211, 157, 59, 0.15)`. Border shifts to `rgba(211, 157, 59, 0.35)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Menus & Drawer Overlays)**: `#141414` backed by a heavy backdrop blur (`blur(24px)`) and a warm bottom shadow `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Metallic Rim Lights**: Premium accents utilize hairlines with directional linear gradients (e.g., `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(240, 214, 173, 0.4) 0%, rgba(211, 157, 59, 0.1) 50%, transparent 100%)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Sharp (`0`)** shape language.

- All primary interactive elements, cards, inputs, and imagery containers have crisp, non-rounded `0px` corners.
- This creates an architectural, modern haute-couture silhouette that echoes Scandinavian modernism and classic velvet club fixtures.
- Geometric tags, badges, and status counters use stark rectangular geometries with hairline borders.
- Imagery is treated as monolithic slabs with razor-sharp crops and subtle interior metallic vignette borders.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary**: Background `#D39D3B`, label `#080808` in `Montserrat` Bold, uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.18em`. Height `3.25rem` (52px), sharp `0px` corners, horizontal padding `2rem`. Hover state transitions background to `#8F6327` with a fine box shadow `0 0 20px rgba(211, 157, 59, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent background, `1px` border in `#F0D6AD`, label `#F0D6AD`. Hover state fills background with `rgba(240, 214, 173, 0.08)` and brightens border to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Tertiary / Link**: Transparent, underline indicator in `#D39D3B` that expands from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Cards & Event Panels

- Base surface `#1A1A1A` enclosed in a hairline border of `#26231E`.
- Padding: `2rem` on desktop, `1.25rem` on mobile.
- Hover: Border elevates to `#8F6327` with a subtle translate-y (`-2px`) and soft golden halo.

### Chips & Badges

- VIP, Class Level (e.g., _On2 Advanced_, _Sensual Bachata_), and Schedule Badges:
- Compact rectangular frames (`height: 1.75rem`), font `label-sm`, uppercase tracking `0.22em`.
- Tone: Background `#121212`, border `1px solid rgba(240, 214, 173, 0.25)`, text `#F0D6AD`.

### Form Fields & Inputs

- Dark recessed background `#121212`, bottom border only (`1px solid #333333`) or 4-sided crisp border (`1px solid #26231E`).
- Text color `#F4F1EA`, placeholder `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.35)`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#D39D3B` with zero outer glow ring, maintaining architectural sharpness. Floating label in `Montserrat` uppercase tracking.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)

- Sharp rectangular boxes (`18px x 18px`), dark background `#121212`, border `#8F6327`.
- Checked state: Filled with `#D39D3B`, containing an angular Cream White (`#F4F1EA`) mark.

### Lists & Timetables

- Studio schedule and instructor rosters appear as hairline-divided rows (`border-bottom: 1px solid #26231E`).
- Time markers in `Bebas Neue` (`#D39D3B`), class names in `Montserrat` bold, instructor names in `Inter` (`#F4F1EA`).
- Row hover reveals a smooth horizontal sweep of `rgba(211, 157, 59, 0.04)`.

### Bespoke Club Components

- **VIP Table & Pass Tier Selector**: Monolithic cards featuring subtle gold top-accent borders (`2px solid #D39D3B`) and Champagne price lockups.
- **Audio / DJ Stream Strip**: Minimalist dark playback bar pinned with hairline gold progress indicators and condensed track titles.
